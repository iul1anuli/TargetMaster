Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Target Master ("the Application") handles user information. Target Master is a single-player game developed as an independent project.

1. Information We Collect

If you choose to sign in using Google OAuth, the Application may receive basic account information authorized by you, strictly limited to:

  • Your Google account display name (for in-game display)
  • Your unique Google user identifier (for account recognition)

Gameplay progress, settings, and high scores may be stored locally on your device or associated with your signed-in profile.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information strictly to:

  • Authenticate your sign-in session
  • Display your player name inside the single-player game
  • Preserve your game progress and respond to support requests

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Authentication is handled securely via Google OAuth services in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain only the minimal information necessary to operate the Application. You may revoke the Application's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, or request deletion of any stored data by contacting us.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request data deletion, please contact: iulianark@gmail.com
